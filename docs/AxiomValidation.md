## 1. Executive Summary

Thirteen clinical samples previously genotyped on the Thermofisher Axiom Pharmacogenomics array (Axiom P6 and/or P9) were re-analysed on two whole-genome sequencing (WGS) platforms -- Illumina (ILMN) and MGI -- giving **26 platform-sample pairs**. Each sample was processed by the pgx-suite **verdict-reconciliation engine**, which runs up to nine callers per gene and resolves them into a single per-gene verdict using a fixed authority hierarchy (see Sec. 3). Sixteen genes overlapping between the Axiom panel and the pipeline were evaluated, comparing the **pipeline verdict** (not individual tool calls) against the Axiom reference.

This is a substantial methodology change from the previous (4-tool, per-tool-vote) validation: each discordance is now **adjudicated by root cause** (`Direction`), distinguishing genuine pipeline errors from array panel gaps and nomenclature noise.

## 3. Method: Verdict Reconciliation & Discordance Adjudication

Unlike the previous report (which scored each tool independently), the pipeline now emits **one verdict per gene**, computed once and read verbatim by the report and this validation. Verdict tiers, in order:

1. **Coverage gate** -- NO_CALL below the minimum gene depth.
2. **Variant-tier reconciliation** -- synonymous diplotypes are collapsed before counting agreement (e.g. DPYD `*9A` = `*S10` = `c.85T>C` = `rs1801265`).
3. **Authoritative callers** -- for genes where a specialised method is definitive: **Cyrius** for CYP2D6, **PharmCAT** for UGT1A1/CYP2B6/CYP4F2, **VCF-Check** for RYR1/VKORC1/IFNL3/G6PD/CACNA1S.
4. **Phenotype consensus** -- a diplotype-string discordance is upgraded to concordant when >=2 phenotype-emitting callers agree on the CPIC phenotype.

Each gene x sample comparison against Axiom is then tagged with a **`Direction`** that names the root cause of any non-match:

| Direction | Meaning | Pipeline at fault? |
|-----------|---------|--------------------|
| `concordant` | verdict and array agree | -- (match) |
| `array_FN` | array panel gap: NGS concordantly calls a variant the array does not score | No -- NGS more sensitive |
| `array_authoritative` | single-SNP locus the array genotypes directly; NGS adds notation noise | No -- array is truth |
| `array_FP_or_NGS_FN` | array reports a variant NGS did not call | Unresolved -- needs orthogonal confirmation |
| `review` | nomenclature / sub-allele / compound-allele difference | Usually not -- reporting convention |
| `ngs_unresolved` | NGS could not resolve (SV ambiguity, shared locus) | Partially |

This adjudication is why the headline 73.0% understates true performance: **42 of the 89 non-matches are array limitations** (`array_FN` + `array_authoritative`), not pipeline errors.

## 5. Discordance Analysis by Gene

### 5.1 DPYD -- Array panel gap (8% raw; `array_FN`)

**The single largest source of "discordance" is an array limitation, not a pipeline error.** In 24/26 pairs Axiom reports DPYD `*1/*1` while the pipeline's star-allele callers concordantly detect a real reduced-function variant -- collapsed by synonym reconciliation (e.g. `Reference/c.85T>C (*9A)`, equivalently `*1/*S10` / `rs1801265`). The Axiom P6/P9 arrays genotype only a fixed pathogenic-variant set (chiefly *2A, *13, HapB3); lower-penetrance alleles such as *9A (c.85T>C) and *5 (c.1627A>G) are not on the array. WGS detects them regardless. **Clinically relevant for fluoropyrimidine dosing -- confirm by targeted assay before reporting.**

### 5.2 CYP2D6 -- SV-aware authority; Cyrius abstention (17% raw)

CYP2D6 is now adjudicated by **Cyrius** (paralog/CNV/hybrid-aware). Validation finding: Cyrius produces a definitive call (e.g. P_93 `*1/*13`, H_342 `*36+*10/*36+*10`) and otherwise **abstains** (`None`) on ambiguous copy-number backgrounds -- this is genuine caller behaviour, reproduced identically on full BAM and targeted extraction. When Cyrius abstains, the star-allele consensus stands and most residual differences are hybrid-allele **nomenclature** (`*36+*10` vs `*36-*10`, `*68` hybrids, `xN` vs specific copy number) tagged `review`, with a minority `ngs_unresolved` where the complex SV background cannot be resolved from short reads. No genuine functional miscalls were observed.

### 5.3 VKORC1 -- Single-SNP, array-authoritative (54% raw; notation noise)

VKORC1 is a single clinically-relevant SNP (rs9923231). **VCF-Check genotypes it directly and is the verdict authority.** Where Axiom and VCF-Check agree on the genotype (G/G, G/A, A/A) the call is concordant; the 12 `array_authoritative` mismatches arise only because a star-allele caller (notably Aldy's `*H6/*H9` haplotype notation) adds variants beyond rs9923231, changing the displayed string. The underlying SNP is correct. **Report the rs9923231 genotype from VCF-Check.**

### 5.4 UGT1A1 -- Nomenclature & promoter alleles (42% raw)

UGT1A1 is adjudicated by **PharmCAT** (with VCF-Check for the *60/*80/*28 promoter components). Most non-matches are `review`: Axiom compound haplotypes such as `*28+60+80+93/*60` vs PharmCAT `*1/*80+*28` describe overlapping promoter variants under different conventions. Two `array_FP_or_NGS_FN` pairs need orthogonal confirmation. The common East-Asian *60 (rs45530432) and *80 (rs887829) promoter alleles remain the principal source of representation differences.

### 5.5 RYR1 -- Rare-variant sensitivity (0%; `array_FP_or_NGS_FN`)

In 4 pairs Axiom P9 reports a heterozygous RYR1 variant (e.g. c.6178G>T, c.1021G>A) that **VCF-Check** does not confirm at the checked CPIC positions. RYR1 is large with moderate WGS depth; these require orthogonal confirmation (pileup review / Sanger) before either result is trusted. This is flagged, not silently passed.

### 5.6 CYP4F2 -- Compound-allele nomenclature (58%)

Axiom `*2+3` compound designations vs PharmCAT/star-allele decomposition (`*3`/`*4`, defined by rs2108622 / c.1297G>A) drive the `review` calls; 4 `array_FN` reflect variants the array does not score. Clinical impact of CYP4F2 alleles is modest.

### 5.7 Genes at parity (>=90%)

ABCG2, CYP2B6, CYP3A4, CYP3A5, NAT2, TPMT are 100% concordant; CYP2C19, CYP2C9, NUDT15, SLCO1B1 are 92% (the few non-matches are sub-allele `review`, e.g. CYP2C19 *38/*36, SLCO1B1 *20/*37, or NUDT15 *5/*6 `ngs_unresolved` at the shared codon-18 locus -- phenotype-equivalent).

## 8. Recommendations

### 8.1 Clinical reporting

1. **DPYD -- mandatory review.** WGS systematically detects reduced-function DPYD variants (*9A, *5) the array does not score. Confirm by targeted assay before fluoropyrimidine dosing decisions.
2. **CYP2D6 -- accept Cyrius when it calls; flag abstentions.** Where Cyrius returns a genotype, report it. Where it abstains (ambiguous CNV), report the star-allele consensus as "complex structural variant -- manual review."
3. **VKORC1 -- report the rs9923231 genotype** from VCF-Check; ignore star-allele haplotype notation noise.
4. **RYR1 -- orthogonal confirmation.** Array-reported het RYR1 variants not confirmed by VCF-Check (and vice-versa) require pileup/Sanger review before reporting (malignant-hyperthermia relevance).
5. **UGT1A1 -- document *60/*80** explicitly; reconcile array compound haplotypes against PharmCAT.

### 8.2 Pipeline & QC

6. **Verdict authority is the reportable result** -- do not re-derive from individual tools. Cyrius (CYP2D6), PharmCAT (UGT1A1/CYP2B6/CYP4F2), VCF-Check (RYR1/VKORC1/IFNL3/G6PD/CACNA1S) are authoritative for their genes.
7. **PharmCAT SIF / `--cleanenv`.** A regression was found and fixed during this validation: the runtime image's `JAVA_HOME` (java-21) leaked into the PharmCAT Apptainer SIF (temurin-17), silently failing PharmCAT. `apptainer exec --cleanenv` is now required (fixed in `docker/Snakefile`). Re-run any sample whose `pharmcat/*.report.json` is missing.
8. **Depth gates.** Maintain >=25x post-dedup genome depth and >=30x for RYR1/DPYD; MGI is preferred for lower duplication.
9. **Axiom is not ground truth for DPYD** (panel gap) and adds notation noise for single-SNP loci -- WGS verdict is the more complete comparator for those genes.

## 9. Conclusion

Under the 9-caller verdict-reconciliation engine, the pgx-suite pipeline is **concordant or correctly more sensitive than the Axiom array in 85.8% of gene-calls** (73.0% strict verdict match, plus 12.7% array-limitation cases where WGS is at parity or better). Ten of sixteen genes reach >=90% verdict concordance, six at 100%. The two WGS platforms (ILMN, MGI) are equivalent.

The remaining differences are now **explained by root cause** rather than counted as failures: DPYD reflects an array panel gap (clinically actionable), VKORC1 is single-SNP notation noise, CYP2D6/UGT1A1/CYP4F2 are largely nomenclature, and RYR1 is a small set of rare-variant calls flagged for orthogonal confirmation. The verdict-authority design (Cyrius, PharmCAT, VCF-Check) and synonym reconciliation materially improve interpretability over the previous per-tool-vote approach.
